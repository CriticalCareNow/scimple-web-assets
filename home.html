Questions

Joint accreditation, answered

What exactly is joint providership? +
Joint providership lets your organization offer accredited CME under Scimple's ACCME accreditation. You bring the educational content and expertise; we provide the accreditation standing and manage all compliance requirements.
Do I need my own accreditation to work with you? +
No — that's the point. You don't need to build or maintain an accreditation department. We carry the accreditation so you can launch certified education immediately.
What does Scimple handle vs. what do I provide? +
You provide the clinical content, faculty, and educational goals. We handle needs assessment, learning objectives, disclosure and conflict management, evaluation, outcomes reporting, certificates, and audit-ready documentation.
Can you help beyond accreditation? +
Absolutely. Complete LMS build-outs (with landing pages and hosting), marketing and advertising, conference organization, enduring online courses, grant and commercial support, outcomes and analytics, faculty development, coaching, video/audio production, and ethical AI integration are all available — individually or as a full package.
How much does it cost? +
Pricing is partnership-first and depends on your project's scope. Reach out for a quick conversation and we'll give you a clear, transparent quote — no obligation.
